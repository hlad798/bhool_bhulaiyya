Bhool Bhulaiyya

The Escape Hunt

You were told never to open the locked door on the third floor of the old haveli. You opened it anyway. Now the corridors twist and fold like a puzzle box, a ghungroo rings in the dark - and Manjulika is awake.

  • Find the glowing gap in the wall to escape each of the 20 levels.
  • Every dead end marked with a red sigil holds a silly riddle. Answer right and the wall crumbles. Answer wrong... she eats you.
  • Each level she wakes sooner and moves faster. Listen to your heartbeat.
  • Move with Arrow keys / WASD, or the buttons on your phone.

Who is brave enough to go in?

Headphones recommended. Sound starts when you enter.

To install on iPhone: tap Share then Add to Home Screen.
